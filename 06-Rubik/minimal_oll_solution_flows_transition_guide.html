Minimal OLL Solution Flows & Transition Guide

Solving the Yellow Face with minimal formula memorization using strictly your 4 core tools.

0. Minimal Formula Collection (Your Toolbox)

  • Sune (1.2): R U R' U R U2' R'
  • Anti-Sune (1.1): R U2' R' U' R U' R'
  • Edge Trigger (ET): F R U R' U' F'
  • Wide Edge Trigger (WT): f R U R' U' f'
