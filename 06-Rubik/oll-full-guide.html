0. Minimal Formula Collection (Your Toolbox)

  • Anti-Sune (OCLL6 / OLL 26): R U2' R' U' R U' R'
  • Edge Trigger (ET / T2 / OLL 45): F R U R' U' F'
  • Wide Edge Trigger (WT): f R U R' U' f'
