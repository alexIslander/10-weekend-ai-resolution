OLL Route Map

Grouping: by landing shape · by first formula

One lane per case, left to right: the case you hold, the setup turn and trigger you need, and the shape you should now be looking at. Every lane ends on the solved yellow face. Lanes are grouped by the shape they land on before their last trigger, so a group is a promise of the form "turn it into this shape, then one trigger finishes it".

0. The four triggers
  • Sune (S): R U R' U R U2' R'
  • Anti-Sune (AS): R U2' R' U' R U' R'
  • Edge Trigger (ET): F R U R' U' F'
  • Wide Edge Trigger (WT): f R U R' U' f'

Click a cube to open that position in the simulator. The colour pickers are the same setting as the step list's, so a recolour here applies there too.

! marks a shape that looks like a case with its own row, but from this lane fewer steps are left than that row needs. Follow the lane; do not jump to the row.

Every shape name and number below is replayed from the step list on a cube model, not typed by hand: if a lane's shape were wrong, the page would fail its check instead of teaching a wrong turn.
