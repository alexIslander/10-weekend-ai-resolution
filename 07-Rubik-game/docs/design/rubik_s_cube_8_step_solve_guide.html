Master the classic 3×3 puzzle from scrambled to solved. Learn the anatomy of the cube, standard move notation, and the two foundational triggers that power all eight steps.

Foundation

The Basics

Center Pieces

1 Colored Tile (6 total). Center pieces never move relative to each other—they anchor each face's color. Throughout this guide, keep Yellow on Top (UP) and White on Bottom (DOWN).

Top ↔ Bottom Opposite Opposite

Edge Pieces

2 Colored Tiles (12 total). Located between the corners on every edge of the cube. Edge pieces always stay edge pieces and connect two adjacent center colors.

Corner Pieces

3 Colored Tiles (8 total). Located at the eight corners of the cube. Corner pieces always stay in corner positions and unite three adjoining faces.

The Moves (Notation Guide)

A single letter means a 90° clockwise turn as if looking directly at that face. An apostrophe (') means 90° counter-clockwise. A 2 means a 180° double turn.

F ↻ Front Clockwise
F' ↺ Front Prime Counter-CW
U ↻ Up (Top) Clockwise
U' ↺ Up Prime Counter-CW
L ↻ Left Clockwise (Down)
L' ↺ Left Prime Counter-CW (Up)
R ↻ Right Clockwise (Up)
R' ↺ Right Prime Counter-CW (Down)
2 ⇄ Double Turn 180° Half-Turn
The Left Trigger Essential Combo

Used throughout Steps 3, 4, and 5 to insert pieces cleanly on the left side.

L'  U'  L
The Right Trigger Essential Combo

The mirror-image combo used on the right side to insert corners and edges.

R  U  R'
Step-by-Step

The 8-Step Solution

1

The Daisy

Goal: 4 white edges around the yellow center
Target UP Face (Daisy)
Hold the cube with the Yellow center on TOP. Bring all four white edge pieces onto the UP face surrounding the yellow center like flower petals.
  • Middle Layer Edges: Rotate the UP face (U' or U) so an empty petal slot sits above the white edge, then turn L' or R to lift it up.
  • Bottom Layer Edges: Align an open petal slot directly above it and execute F2.
  • Flipped Edges: Turn F or F' to move the edge into the middle layer, then lift it into an open slot.
2

The White Cross

Goal: White cross on bottom aligned with centers
Target DOWN Face
Keep Yellow on TOP. Look at the side color attached to one of your four white daisy petals.
  1. Rotate the UP face (U or U') until that edge's side color matches its matching center piece on the front face.
  2. Once matched, turn that front face 180° (F2) to send the white edge down to the bottom layer.
  3. Repeat for all 4 white petals to form the White Cross on the bottom.
Send Matched Edge to Bottom F2
3

The White Corners

Goal: Complete the entire white bottom face
Solved DOWN Face
Find a corner piece in the top layer that contains a white tile. Rotate U until it sits directly above its matching two center colors.
  • White faces Front-Left: Use the Left Trigger (L' U' L).
  • White faces Front-Right: Use the Right Trigger (R U R').
  • White faces UP (Top): Position above an unsolved corner and run R U R' twice (R U R' R U R') so white faces the side, then insert normally.
Left / Right Corner Insert
L' U' L R U R'
4

The Middle Layer

Goal: Solve the first two layers (F2L)

Find an edge piece on the UP face without yellow. Rotate U to match its front tile with its center piece to form an upside-down "T". Look at its top color to see if it needs to go Left or Right:

Move Edge Left (then re-insert white corner) U' L' U  +  L' U' L
Move Edge Right (then re-insert white corner) U R U'  +  R U R'
Solving Note (Trapped Edges): If a middle-layer edge is stuck in the wrong slot or flipped, perform either Move Left or Move Right once to pop it out into the UP layer, then solve it normally.
5

The Yellow Cross

Goal: Form a yellow cross on the UP face
1. Dot (Any Angle)
2. L-Shape (Top-Left)
3. Horizontal Line

Ignore the yellow corners for now. Match your UP face to one of the three states above (hold the L-shape at top-left, or the Line horizontally) and repeat the algorithm until the Yellow Cross appears:

F + Right Trigger + U' F' F  R U R'  U' F'
6

The Yellow Face

Goal: Orient all yellow tiles onto the UP face
0 Corners (Yellow Left)
1 Corner ("Fish" Bottom-Left)
2 Corners (Yellow Front-Left)
  • 0 Yellow Corners: Ensure a yellow corner tile faces Left on the bottom-left corner.
  • 1 Yellow Corner ("Fish"): Put the single yellow corner at the bottom-left ("fish nose").
  • 2 Yellow Corners: Place a yellow corner sticker facing Front-Left.
Repeat Until UP Face is Solid Yellow R  U  R'  U  R  U2  R'
7

The Yellow Corners

Goal: Position all 4 top corners accurately
Matching Corners on Left
Rotate U to find two adjacent corners on the same side that share the same color. Hold those matching corners on the LEFT face (if none match yet, run the algorithm once from any side).

Execute the corner-swap sequence below, followed immediately by Step 6's algorithm to restore the yellow top face:

Part A: Swap Corners + Part B: Step 6 Algorithm L' U R U' L U R'  +  R U R' U R U2 R'
8

The Yellow Edges

Goal: Permute final edges to solve the cube!
Solved Face at BACK
Check if one of the four side faces is completely solved. If yes, place that solved face at the BACK. If no side is solved yet, hold the cube with any side facing you.

Execute the final edge-cycling algorithm up to 3 times until every face is completely solved!

Final Edge Cycle (Repeat up to 3x) F2  U  L  R'  F2  L'  R  U  F2
